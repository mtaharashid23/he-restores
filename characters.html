Meet the Story

He Restores is a true story. These are the voices that carried it — presented here so you can connect with them before you meet them in full on the page.

The Narrator

Denise

“I did not survive to stay silent.”
Her Struggle

Carrying the hurt and hopelessness she witnessed inside her own family, and inside families she counseled.

Her Transformation

From surviving to restoring — becoming a voice for healing, identity, and transformation through Christ.

Her Husband — 42 Years of Marriage

The Veteran

“Restoration is a promise we kept, one season at a time.”
His Struggle

Building a life and a marriage across the demands of military service and the ordinary weight of family life.

His Transformation

A steady, faithful presence — proof that commitment, given enough grace, becomes restoration.

Two Adult Children · Six Grandchildren

The Next Generation

“Every generation after us gets to inherit what was rebuilt.”
Their Inheritance

A family that chose restoration over repetition — and grandchildren growing up inside that healing.

Their Role

Living proof of what Denise writes about: that what God restores in one generation reaches the next.

The Quiet Presence Throughout

Grace

“He restores what the years have taken.”
Where She Shows Up

In every chapter — not as a character exactly, but as the reason the story doesn’t end in Brokenness.

What She Offers the Reader

Permission to believe restoration is available to you too, not just to Denise.

Their Story, Waiting for You

Come meet them fully — inside the book.
