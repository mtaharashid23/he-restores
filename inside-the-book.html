Inside the Book

Not a synopsis — a walk through the themes that hold He Restores together.

I. Faith

A belief carried through the dark

Faith, in this book, is not certainty. It is the decision to keep believing when the evidence says otherwise. Denise traces the moments her faith felt smallest — and how those became the moments it grew the most.

II. Family

The people worth fighting to restore

Married more than 42 years, raising two children into adults and watching six grandchildren inherit a family healed instead of a family broken — family is both the wound and the reward in He Restores.

III. Healing

What grace looks like, up close

Denise holds certifications in Life Coaching, Grief Coaching, and Mental Health Coaching — and it shows. This section reads like both memoir and gentle guide for anyone standing in their own healing season.

IV. Purpose

Finding why you were made for this moment

A Master’s in Human Services Counseling and dual degrees in Psychology inform this thread — but it is Denise’s own restoration that turns purpose from theory into testimony.

V. Restoration

Where the story — and you — begin again

The final thread ties every earlier one together: restoration is not the absence of what was broken, but proof of what God can rebuild from it.
