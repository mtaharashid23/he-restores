FAQ

Everything you might want to know before you begin reading — or sharing this book with someone you love.

Is He Restores a memoir or a novel?

He Restores is Denise C. Herndon Harvey’s deeply personal, true story — written with the emotional pacing of a narrative so it reads like a journey, not a report.

What formats is the book available in?

He Restores is available in paperback, hardcover, and Kindle, with an audiobook coming in the future. Visit the Buy page for current retailer links.

Is this book faith-based?

Yes. He Restores is rooted in Christian faith and centers on God’s grace, love, and power to restore what life has taken.

Can I use this book for a book club or church group?

Absolutely — many book clubs and church discussion groups have used He Restores as a guided study on forgiveness, healing, and family restoration. Visit the Community page for examples.

Is this appropriate for readers who are not religious?

While the book is faith-centered, its themes of hope, healing, and family restoration speak to anyone walking through a difficult season.

How can I book Denise to speak?

Visit the About page for booking inquiries, or reach out directly through the contact details in the footer.

Can I share my own restoration story?

Yes — we built an entire page for that. Visit Share Your Restoration Story to submit your testimony, photos, or prayer request.

Where can I find more of Denise’s work?

Denise is also the author of Growing Up Sassafras: Where Is My Daddy? and Emergence of Me: Discovering My Identity and Courage Within, along with contributions to several Amazon bestselling devotionals.
